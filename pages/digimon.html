How It Works

Raise a Digimon from an egg in real time: feed it, train it, clean up after it, put it to bed and battle through the 100-round Colosseum. The pet lives on your clock, so time keeps passing while this tab is closed.

Care Guide VER.20TH
  • hungerfeed meat to fill its hunger hearts
  • strengthprotein fills strength hearts (and adds weight)
  • trainingraises effort; how you care for it shapes what it evolves into
  • clean upflush the poop — leave it too long and it gets sick
  • lightsturn the lights off when it falls asleep
  • callsthe call light means it needs something; ignoring it is a care mistake
  • battlefight through the Colosseum; patch injuries with medicine
