      §01 At a glance deepdish_ocs @ 925ea62 · 2026-11-06

      LIVE RENDER · ARC REACTOR

      OCS turns a Gakken Worldeye (a little projector that paints onto the inside of a frosted sphere) into an eye that watches the room. A webcam finds people. The eye follows them, reacts with expressions, drifts through moods when nobody is around, and takes over the sphere for the hour, a siren, or a real aircraft passing overhead.

      Every control goes through one HTTP API, so the same things can be done from the desktop GUI, the web UI at :5001, the touch UI at /mobile, curl, Home Assistant, or the wheel of a BUSY Bar on the desk.

      §04 Render pipeline OpenCV + NumPy · back to front

      Each frame is painted from the back of the eye forward. Frames are cached for 33 ms while active (30 fps) and 120 ms idle; low-memory mode uses 50 / 200 ms. Fills build their layers one color channel at a time (three whole-plane NumPy operations beat broadcasting against a 3-element color) and counter-rotate through cached remaps.

      §05 Behavior & mood valence · arousal · dominance

      The mood is a V / A / D vector that decays toward a baseline. Stimuli (a face appearing, motion, idle time, manual expressions) nudge it, and the mood biases which behaviors fire and how strongly. The last 30 stimuli are kept as a feed for the web UI.

      valence−1 … +1 · base 0.05 · decay 0.020/ssad, angry ↔ happy, content
      arousal0 … 1 · base 0.30 · decay 0.040/scalm, sleepy ↔ excited, alert
      dominance0 … 1 · base 0.50 · decay 0.015/stimid ↔ confident

      Behavior tree reactive expressions on: each tick scores what holds

      behaviorfires whendoescooldown
      WelcomeBacka face returns after >25 s away (or >40 s idle)surprised (or angry)30 s
      EnjoyingFacea face tracked for >4 shappy 3.5 s180 s
      ConfusedByMotionno face, motion >0.35confused 3 s15 s
      SuspiciousMotionno face for >10 s, motion 0.10–0.35suspicious 3 s25 s
      IdleMoodno face, no movement for >20 sbored or sad 4 s25 s
      CuriousScancurious mood, nothing to focus onquick searching look60 s

      Expressions · 8 0.5–10 s at 0–1 intensity · each pushes the mood by ΔV ΔA ΔD × intensity

      Rendered on the Sentinel preset, 0.8 s in. Overlays ease in over the first 12% and out over the last 25%; effects that belong to the eye follow the pupil.

      §06 Tracking best backend that works, and why the others didn't

      #backendmodeldevice
      1YOLOyolov8n-face.pt, else yolo11n.pt (person)CUDA
      2YOLOsame modelsCPU
      3MediaPipeface detectionCPU
      4OpenCVHaar frontalface_defaultCPU, last resort

      Each faster backend that fails logs why, which is how CUDA driver / wheel mismatches and broken MediaPipe builds get diagnosed. On top of detection: smoothed gaze toward the locked face, face-lock dynamics, idle wandering with dwells and sweeps, a smoothed motion average for the behavior tree, and a surprised reaction (with cooldown) when a new face appears.

      /api/calibration tunes the gaze mapping; /api/tracking toggles it; --no-face-tracking starts without it.

      §07 Alerts & takeovers one at a time · wall-clock timed

      Flyover monitor src/services/flyover.py

      Polls community ADS-B APIs (no key) for aircraft near home. A new aircraft entering the zone below the ceiling triggers the flyover, with its bearing from home and vertical rate passed to the animation. A flyover is refused while the siren is up.

      sourcesadsb.lol, then airplanes.live (API-compatible fallback)
      zone3.0 nm radius · below 15,000 ft
      pollevery 15 s on a background thread
      locationlat / lon, or a zip code geocoded via zippopotam.us (flyover_config.json)

      Thinking

      /api/eye/thinking holds a thinking state (orbiting loader dots, counter-rotating dash ring) while a chat or LLM reply is pending.

      Clips: Arc Reactor preset, 3 s of each at 12 fps.

      OCS drives a BUSY Bar as a desk companion: its 72×16 RGB front matrix shows one page at a time (the rail on the left says which), its 160×80 back screen a full status sheet. The bar's own HTTP API takes draws grouped under deepdish_ocs; its protobuf status stream (wheel, buttons, power, brightness) is decoded by hand from the bar's .proto field numbers, so OCS needs no protobuf dependency.

      Eye window placement

      Window managers open new windows on the focused monitor, so src/app/window_placement.py saves where the eye went fullscreen (eye_window.json) and puts it back on the projector at the next start. Monitors are identified through Hyprland (hyprctl), falling back to the newest live instance when a long-lived shell carries a stale signature; other window managers use the saved position.
